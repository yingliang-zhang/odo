- Design-MoA is gated behind prefs `design_via: moa` through `resolveVia`; absent, explicit `omp`, or garbage values all fail closed before any moa call; fanout is 3 blind concurrent `QueryWithTools` legs (models from prefs `review:`, same goal+context, repo-root-scoped FS executor, 16-round tool cap, per-leg `moa.TimeoutForModel`), then a single consolidator `moa.Query` that synthesizes (not votes) over labeled Leg A/B/C inputs (moa-chain-epoch-2)
- Truncation policy (stricter than spec text): a truncated leg is a failed leg — its partial text is dropped and never reaches the consolidator, but its receipts are kept; the pipeline degrades on surviving legs and dies only when every leg fails; a truncated consolidator fails the command closed with no `design_lock` row, and a degraded pass must not emit a failure marker (moa-chain-epoch-2) (moa-chain-epoch-3)
