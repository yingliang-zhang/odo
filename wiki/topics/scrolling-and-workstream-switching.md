# Scrolling & Workstream Switching

- Workstream-switch scroll bug: `applyBootstrap` wholesale-replaces `events` but `ChatSurface` does not remount, so a `stickRef=false` from the previous session leaked across and the new session stayed off-bottom; fix resets stick/pill/pinToBottom on conversationId change with ResizeObserver re-anchoring; the regression test needs unequal-length histories (12 vs 72 messages) because equal lengths coincidentally land at the bottom and false-pass (bug-fix-epoch-2)
- Auto-follow semantics: un-stick only on explicit user gestures (wheel-up excluding the ToolTicker inner scroll, touch pull-down, scrollbar drag), never on scroll events — browser scroll anchoring and `content-visibility` size resolution move `scrollTop` passively and were being misread as user intent; programmatic writes get a 250 ms suppression and ResizeObserver re-pins invisible growth (UI-epoch-2)
- Switch lag had four root causes — full journal replay per bootstrap, daemon global lock held over SQL/file I/O, unconditional `generateAgentsMD` rewrite, no GUI-side cache; fixed with a per-conversation LRU stale-while-revalidate cache + seq merge, incremental bootstrap via `afterSeq`/`conversationId` (full replay only as cold fallback), snapshot-in-lock/I/O-outside-lock, and content-identical write skip (UI-epoch-8)
- The optimistic switch flip got a real rollback after review caught the incomplete one: a `rootFlipped` boolean computed at flip time, restored workstream refs, `handleSend` in-flight responses guarded by captured `(cid, root)`, and no ref mutation inside state updaters; no before/after latency numbers were captured, so smoothness improvement is test-evidenced but unmeasured (UI-epoch-8)
- The switch-cache diff's auto-land was blocked at the time by a failing rollback-on-failure e2e (plus two stale pre-existing reds); the work later landed as diff #22 after its snapshot was rebased byte-identical, the stale tests fixed, and main verified green (UI-epoch-8) (UI-epoch-10)
