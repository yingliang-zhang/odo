# Panel Review & Settlement Doctrine

- Three-round rule: review rounds 1–2 require unanimous accept (fail-closed veto retained); round 3 (terminal) passes on a majority valve requiring ≥2/3 accept with zero reject/infra/truncated legs, else suspend for human (main-epoch-21)
- Gate-source diffs never land via the majority valve — they route to ladder_suspended with human accept as fallback, and the majority_accept attestation path for gate files was deleted; a 2/3 panel must not be able to land its own gate weakening (main-epoch-14)
- D7 verdict policy: ≥2 reject legs from ≥2 distinct model families auto-rejects (reject_independent); exactly 1 reject or ≥2 same-family rejects suspends as panel_minority_reject with the diff staying pending for inbox triage (reject_minority); verify failures never auto-reject (bug-fix-epoch-17)
- Model family = basename prefix before the first '-', lowercase-folded (modelspec.Family); single-family panels have zero auto-reject capacity — everything minority-suspends, fail-closed and visible (bug-fix-epoch-17)
- panel_minority_reject is non-terminal while repanel_count < 2 (restart recovery re-fires once with a fresh panel); at ≥2 it is parked human-only; the count is journal-derived and fails closed to parked on read failure (bug-fix-epoch-17)
- panel_mixed no longer triggers instant auto-reject — divergent verdicts enter the repair ladder; if divergence persists past 3 review-and-fix rounds, strict majority (>½ accepts) decides, else suspend for human (main-epoch-19)
- A truncated leg is forced to needs_fixes at construction (reviewVerdict) and invalidates the majority valve — conservative fail-closed default retained deliberately (main-epoch-21)
- A single configured review model disarms auto-land entirely (single-judge panel cannot attest), with a once-per-daemon-lifetime advisory; the review_diff advisory surface is deliberately not capped (main-epoch-15)
- Panel legs, the reviewWithModel funnel, and /vision//preview legs all run under an outer WithTimeout(legTimeout) deadline — all were previously unbounded goroutines (main-epoch-15)
- D2 grounded reviewer leg: exactly one repo-grounded leg per fan-out, with a daemon-computed one-hop import-neighborhood scope hashed as scope_sha16; every tool read attempt (allowed or denied) is journaled in tool_calls before output returns, making cite-without-calling test-detectable (bug-fix-epoch-18)
- Grounded-leg fees: gate-source diffs require a functioning grounded leg (default grounded_review_required=gate_sources); a degraded grounded leg on gate source = Infra → round fails closed; budget exhaustion still owes a verdict (journaled tool_budget_exhausted); empty scope (base==HEAD) is legal, not infra (bug-fix-epoch-18)
- No panel consolidator is ever added: divergence is the product; answers remain raw per-leg concatenation because a 'complete-looking synthesis' deceives more than raw legs (main-epoch-14)
- Manual Accept on gate-source diffs legitimately bypasses the unanimous-panel attestation requirement when the block reason bound to a stale sha after repack — terminal-state diffs never auto-retry, so human accept is the designed unlock (main-epoch-41)
- Manual-accept evidence discipline: before accepting, verify archive sha16 consistency across journal rows and disk recompute, attribute the payload by grep, and prove same-bytes reproduction green — a positive rerun under identical bytes has zero information gain (main-epoch-36)
- Panel claims are adjudicated by triage against source, not by trusting the verdict: glm's three rejection charges on #41 were refuted code-by-code and the diff was manually landed with a ledger_correction journal row (corrects_seq) rather than a silent DB mutation (main-epoch-27)
