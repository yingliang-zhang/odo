# Review Panel & Verdict Governance

- Three-round rule: rounds 1–2 require unanimous accept (fail-closed one-vote veto); the terminal round-3 evaluation falls to the majority valve; implemented as settleMaxReviseRounds 3→2 (main-epoch-21)
- The majority valve's only reachable composition is {2 accept, 1 needs_fixes} — a reject leg auto-rejects before the ladder and an infra leg blocks before evaluation; truncated legs invalidate the valve (main-epoch-21)
- panel_mixed enters the repair ladder rather than auto-rejecting; divergence past the round cap is decided by strict majority (>1/2) of accepts, else suspended for humans (main-epoch-19)
- Truncated legs are forced to needs_fixes at verdict construction and additionally abstained in the cap-count loop as local self-proof against future producer regressions (main-epoch-20)
- D7 verdict policy: only ≥2 reject legs from ≥2 distinct model families auto-reject (reject_independent); a single reject or same-family rejects suspend via panel_minority_reject with at most two restart recovery re-panels before parking human-only; single-family panels have zero auto-reject capacity (bug-fix-epoch-17)
- D6 design-gate diversity: the auto design gate requires ≥2 successful legs from ≥2 distinct model families (family derived from the bare model id via modelspec.Family, not provider labels); refusal journals auto_gate:'refused_diversity' and left exactly the human-gate fold state, while manual /design_moa and endpoint-honest scrubBaseURL receipts stay unchanged (bug-fix-epoch-21)
- D2 grounded reviewer: exactly one repo-grounded leg per fan-out with equal verdict weight; scope is the one-hop import neighborhood hashed as scope_sha16; every tool call including refusals is journaled into tool_calls; a degraded grounded leg on a gate-source diff fails closed to infra (bug-fix-epoch-18)
- Gate-source diffs never land via the majority valve: attestation accepts only consensus:'accept'; panelVerdictAttestsDiff binds the verdict to landing bytes via patch_sha16 (main-epoch-14)
- With only one review model configured the panel is not armed; a once-per-daemon-lifetime single_judge_panel advisory is emitted (main-epoch-15)
- Blocking panel claims are adjudicated per-charge against source before accept: e.g. the sameAutoDistillList duplicate-id hole was valid and fixed with consume semantics, while the memo-instability and Lstat-bypass charges were rejected with code-level counter-evidence (main-epoch-33)
- autoland block precedence puts run-state evidence (run_errored, tainted verdicts) before configuration advisories like the single-judge check, so an errored single-model run no longer burns its once-per-lifetime quota on the wrong reason (main-epoch-18)
- Journal ledger_correction rows (with corrects_seq) are the only accepted repair for store/git divergence — silent DB mutation is rejected by convention (main-epoch-27)
