# Auto-Land Pipeline & Diff Lifecycle

- Terminal blocked states (verify_failed, panel_mixed, protected_path) never auto-retry; manual Accept is the only unlock, backed by a long precedent chain (#41, #42, #45, #46, #48, #49, #52) (main-epoch-41)
- panel_infra is the sole non-terminal blocked reason; a daemon restart re-fires the pending diff through recover-pending-diffs (main-epoch-17)
- The repair chain is permanently closed above a 64KB diff body (hard cap, 'no silent truncation'); oversized diffs with a needs_fixes consensus route to human Accept only (main-epoch-33)
- Auto-revision products supersede their origins: reject the origin diff first so a restart cannot re-fire its panel against the newer product (diffs #51/#52) (main-epoch-41)
- Accept-time dirty-path guards refuse when patch-own paths carry staged/unstaged/untracked content; the refusal is deliberately NOT wrapped as errBaseStale because user dirt needs human triage, not another ~8.5min auto-revise cycle (main-epoch-28)
- ProbeAlreadyLanded runs before the dirty-path refusal, so unstaged edits identical to the patch land via bookkeeping instead of being killed (main-epoch-28)
- retireRun selects the diff's own run by worktreePath match with byConv only as fallback; reviewing an older diff no longer closes a newer run's worktree or kills an in-flight verify (main-epoch-28)
- Rejecting a diff deletes its candidate worktree; rescueResolvedWorktree snapshots the current delta to .odo/diffs/<run>-rescue.diff before retirement, but only when it differs from the archived patch (main-epoch-36)
- Memory-path diffs (wiki/, .odo/) are refused at registration by a drainRun guard between ExtractDiff and InsertDiff, with an advisory pointing at the distill/wiki-commit route; rejectMemoryPaths refuses them for every actor including human Accept (main-epoch-24)
- The epoch-42 backend patch was rejected at registration for touching wiki/topics/memory-distill.md; the full patch is preserved at .odo/diffs/6a8d8993-68af79915939.diff and must be stripped of wiki hunks and resent — wiki corrections land only through the daemon's own distill/wiki-commit pipeline, never agent edits (main-epoch-42)
- Gate-source diffs (settle.go/autoland.go family) bypass majority auto-land: they require unanimous panel attestation or a human Accept (main-epoch-22)
- Patch regeneration per drain semantics (git diff --cached HEAD) before accept is safe only when the diff never reached a panel — no patch_sha16 attestation binding exists to break (main-epoch-33)
- Revise runs that staged their work in the origin worktree previously wedged the ladder in silent pending; drainRun now journals a loud revise_product_missing row, and repair/rebase prompts declare the run's own worktree as the canonical checkout (bug-fix-epoch-13)
- Schema v3 stores the review goal at InsertDiff; recover/review_diff/loop-seed/revise consume the stored goal, ending the false 'objective mismatch' rejections that wrongfully killed diff #34 (main-epoch-18)
- Orphaned session-worktree commits (a5c98ca, abca6f1) were cherry-picked onto main after discovery that 'landed' work never reached main; store rejected statuses are left untouched with ledger_correction journal rows as the reconciliation mechanism (main-epoch-20)
